What Is The GC's Office?

A free, web-first tool built for working contractors — GCs, subs, every trade. Estimates, contracts, bookkeeping, receipts, subs, change orders, lien waivers, permits, job photos — all in one place, all connected, all free.

No subscriptions. No per-project fees. No paywalls. Free forever.

The Problem We're Solving

Contractors run their business out of their truck, their head, or a messy spreadsheet. They type the same number three times — once for the bid, once for the contract, once for the expense tracker. Subs send invoices by text. GCs log them manually. Nobody knows if the job is making money until it's over.

This eliminates all of it.

Who It's For

General Contractor

Prefilled estimates from real material costs and regional labor averages. Bid converts to a signable contract. Signed contract becomes an active job-cost ledger automatically.

Subcontractor — Any Trade

Claim your profile free. Get a trade-scoped bid tool. Send your signed contract directly to the GC — it posts to their ledger automatically. Zero double-entry.

Homeowner

Get a real number before work starts. No sticker shock. Every line item broken out so the estimate is a conversation, not a confrontation.

The Rolodex — Find a Pro

The page you're on right now. Seeded from California's public contractor licensing registry — 275,000+ licensed contractors. Every profile shows verified license status, bond status, and workers' comp coverage. Claimed profiles unlock direct call and text.

If you're a licensed contractor, your profile is already here. Claim it free in 2 minutes.
